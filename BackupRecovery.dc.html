Understanding Backup & Recovery

Backup that is clean, fast and always recoverable

A backup is only useful if you can restore from it — and if what you restore is clean. Our approach combines reliable cloud and local backup with integrated anti-malware, so every backup is scanned and you never reintroduce ransomware or infected files during a recovery.

Backups run automatically on a schedule you define, with flexible retention and both offsite cloud and local copies. That means you are protected against everything from an accidental file deletion to a full site loss, and can recover at the speed the situation demands.

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and retention policies can be aligned to your regulatory and audit requirements — turning backup from an insurance policy into a genuine business-continuity capability.
