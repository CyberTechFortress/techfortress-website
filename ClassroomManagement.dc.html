Understanding Classroom Management

A complete suite for safe, effective learning

A full Classroom Management solution gives schools everything needed to monitor, guide and support student devices from a single dashboard — going beyond a single point tool to cover the whole learning environment.

Safeguarding is built in: content and policy controls keep students protected online, enforcing acceptable-use rules automatically so staff can focus on teaching rather than policing devices.

It is designed to serve both audiences in a school — simple enough for teachers to use in the flow of a lesson, and powerful enough for the IT team responsible for managing and securing the estate behind the scenes.
