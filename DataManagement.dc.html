Understanding Data Management

Protect, retain and recover your most valuable asset

Data is the lifeblood of a modern business, and managing it well means keeping it protected, compliant and available at all times. Our Data Management brings together the capabilities needed to achieve that across its whole lifecycle.

That means reliable backup with built-in anti-malware so data is always recoverable and clean, disaster recovery so operations continue through any outage, and data loss prevention so sensitive information never leaves the business improperly.

It also means secure, managed collaboration through Microsoft 365 and file sharing — so your teams can work with data productively while it stays protected and under your control.
