Understanding Endpoint Security

Every device is a door — secure them all

Endpoints — laptops, desktops, servers and mobile devices — are where most attacks land, because they are where your people work. Endpoint Security is the coordinated set of protections that defends every one of those devices against the full range of cyber threats.

Rather than a single product, effective endpoint security is a layered approach: advanced email security to stop threats arriving, EDR to detect and respond to what gets through, managed monitoring to provide expert oversight, and ransomware-specific protection for the highest-impact threats.

Delivered together and managed centrally, these layers give you defence in depth — so that if one control is bypassed, others stand behind it, and every incident is captured with full forensics.
