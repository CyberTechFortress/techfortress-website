Understanding File Sync & Share

Secure collaboration from anywhere

Modern teams need to reach their files from any device and any location, and to collaborate on them in real time. Advanced File Sync and Share makes that possible while keeping the data properly secured — a safer, more manageable alternative to consumer file-sharing tools.

Access is controlled with granular permissions, and data is encrypted, so sensitive files are shared only with the right people and remain protected wherever they travel. Changes sync automatically across devices, so everyone is always working from the current version.

The result is a productivity boost without a security trade-off: teams stop emailing files back and forth and start collaborating properly, while IT retains visibility and control.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How is this safer than consumer tools?

Access is controlled with granular permissions and encryption, and IT retains visibility and control over shared data.

Can we work across multiple devices?

Yes. Files sync automatically across all your devices so you always have the current version.

Who can access shared files?

Only the people you grant permission to — sharing is controlled at a granular level.

Does it replace emailing files around?

Yes. Teams collaborate on live files instead of sending versions back and forth by email.
