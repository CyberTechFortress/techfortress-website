Safe adoption of generative AI

Generative AI boosts productivity — but employees pasting confidential data into public AI tools can expose customer records, source code and trade secrets. Blocking AI outright is not realistic; the goal is to use it safely.

GenAI Protection gives you visibility and control over how AI tools are used across your organization, so you get the productivity without the data-loss and compliance risk.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Why do we need GenAI protection?

Employees use AI tools daily, often pasting sensitive data into them. This protects that data and keeps AI use compliant — without banning the tools.

Does it block AI tools completely?

It can, but the goal is safe enablement — allowing approved tools while blocking risky data from leaving your organization.

Which AI tools does it cover?

It provides visibility and control across popular generative AI tools and apps used in the workplace, such as ChatGPT and Copilot.

How does it help with compliance?

It enforces policies on AI data use and provides reporting, helping you meet GDPR and other regulatory requirements.
