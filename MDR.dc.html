Understanding MDR

Security operations, delivered as a service

Managed Detection and Response combines technology and human expertise to monitor your environment around the clock. A Security Operations Centre (SOC) team watches for threats, investigates alerts, and responds on your behalf — effectively giving you a full security team without the cost and difficulty of building one in-house.

The value of MDR is not just detection but response. Most breaches cause damage in the gap between an alert firing and someone acting on it. Our analysts close that gap, triaging every alert, hunting proactively for threats, and containing incidents fast.

MDR integrates with the security tools you already have, adding expert oversight and a clear escalation path so that when something serious happens, it is handled quickly and correctly.
