Understanding M365 Collaboration

Getting the most from Microsoft 365 — safely

Microsoft 365 is central to how modern teams work, but its default settings rarely match a business's security and collaboration needs. We configure and manage your tenant so that file sync, sharing, Teams and email work seamlessly while being properly secured.

A common and costly misconception is that Microsoft backs up your data for you. Native retention is limited, so we add proper backup and protection for your M365 data, keeping it recoverable well beyond the defaults.

We also help you automate repetitive work using the tools already in your licence — streamlining approvals, notifications and routine processes with Power Automate so your team spends less time on manual busywork.
