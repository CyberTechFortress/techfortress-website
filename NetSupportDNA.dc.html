NetSupport DNA (Corporate Edition)

IT and network management

Within NetSupport DNA, network managers gain a wealth of IT asset-management features to effectively track, monitor and manage IT assets and endpoints across a business — from one central point.

Armed with a complete overview of IT activity, NetSupport DNA helps technicians work smarter, maintaining a secure and reliable network. From staying ahead of potential issues before they escalate, to onboarding tools or viewing a handy software distribution status report, DNA gathers a wealth of device and usage data to inform decision making and drive smarter planning of talent, IT spending and refresh cycles.

NetSupport DNA (Education Edition)

IT and network management for schools

NetSupport DNA provides schools with the tools to manage technology in the classroom and across the school while safeguarding students and supporting teachers.

Easy to install and use, it offers a wealth of network and IT-management tools for network managers to track, monitor and manage IT assets and endpoints across a school or online trust district — all from one central point, with built-in safeguarding features that provide both proactive and reactive tools to further enhance a school's safeguarding policies.
