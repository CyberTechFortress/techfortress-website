Understanding NetSupport Manager

The remote support standard, proven over 30 years

NetSupport Manager is one of the most established remote-control and support solutions available, used by IT teams worldwide to connect to and support devices quickly and securely. Whether a user is in the next room or on another continent, a technician can take control and resolve issues in seconds.

Security is central: sessions are protected with strong encryption and granular permissions, so remote access is tightly controlled and fully auditable. It works reliably across LAN, WAN and internet connections, and across every major platform.

As a managed offering, we deploy, configure and support NetSupport Manager for you, so your team gets dependable remote support without the setup and maintenance burden.
