Understanding Network Security

Your network is the front door — we lock it

Your network is the gateway to everything your business runs on, which makes it a primary target. Network Security protects that gateway with properly designed and configured firewalls, network segmentation, and access controls that keep unauthorised users out while giving legitimate users the access they need.

Attackers look for weaknesses, so we look for them first. Continuous vulnerability scanning identifies misconfigurations and unpatched systems before they can be exploited, and traffic monitoring gives you clear visibility of what is actually happening on your network.

Segmentation is a particularly powerful defence: by dividing the network into zones, a breach in one area is contained rather than spreading across the whole business. Combined with secure remote access, this gives you both protection and flexibility.
