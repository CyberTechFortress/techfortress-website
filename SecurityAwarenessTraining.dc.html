FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Why do we need security awareness training?

The vast majority of breaches involve human error. Training turns employees from your biggest vulnerability into an active layer of defense.

How much time does it take employees?

Lessons are short and engaging — usually a few minutes — and delivered on a schedule that fits around work, so training sticks without disruption.

What are phishing simulations?

Safe, simulated phishing emails sent to your staff to test awareness. Anyone who clicks is guided to a quick training moment — no blame, just learning.

Does it help with compliance and insurance?

Yes. Regular training and reporting help meet regulatory obligations and increasingly common cyber-insurance requirements.
